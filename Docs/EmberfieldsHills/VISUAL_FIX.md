# Emberfields hills visual fix

Install on top of the Emberfields Hills update. Exit Play Mode, merge the Assets folder, and choose **AshenVale → Map layouts → Fix Emberfields hills**. The installer checks the expected source before writing and saves the prior scripts in EmberfieldsVisualFixBackups.

Emberfields has two permanent exits. The west gate leads to Emberhaven and the east gate leads to Amberleaf Trail. The road uses the existing autumn ground art on a separate sloped strip. The scenery is cropped above the old flat road and fitted uniformly to cover the camera's full climb. This can make the scenery look closer than in the previous version.

After installing, check the west entrance, walk through the central hollow, and climb to the highest branch to inspect the backdrop. Local checks covered C# syntax, repeated installation, the two gate entries, and the camera coverage calculation. Visual alignment and Unity compilation need a Play Mode test.
