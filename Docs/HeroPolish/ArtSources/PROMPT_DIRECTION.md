# Prompt directions used for this art pass

Style reference: approved Rimecaller concept — polished hand painted pixel-like chibi 2D side-scroller characters, transparent backgrounds, full bodies, consistent head size, generous cell gutters, no text/grid/scenery.

Knight base: red/black armor, crimson dragon-scale plates, gold trim, tousled black hair, red split cape, long red/black/silver spear; 4x4 atlas of walk, idle, jump/fall, thrust/sweep, leap/land, hurt/death.

Ranger base: youthful blond male elf, pointed ears, green/gold cape/tunic, brown leather boots/gloves/quiver, curved green wooden bow; 4x4 atlas of walk, idle, jump/fall, draw/release/recovery, multishot, hurt/death.

Utility sheets: 4x3 atlases for each exact class identity: four right-facing relaxed walking poses carrying the weapon vertically, four back-view climbing poses alternating raised arms/knees, and four prone transition poses from kneeling to lying flat.

Mage climbing: 2x2 atlas of the exact navy/silver/cyan mage, back view, left arm high/right low, arms middle, right arm high/left low, arms middle; alternating lifted knees, no ladder drawn, no crystal glow.

Ranger climbing refinement: same 2x2 pose template as mage climb, retaining blond elf/green gold/bow/quiver identity. Explicit square back view and opposite high arm in frames1/3.

All sheets mechanically sliced and uniformly scaled; grounded frames translated to shared boot contact row without stretching the character.
